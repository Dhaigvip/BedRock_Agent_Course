VISA REQUIREMENTS REFERENCE — POPULAR TRAVEL DESTINATIONS
==========================================================

This document summarises visa requirements for citizens of major passport-holding
countries (USA, UK, Canada, Australia, EU/Schengen) visiting popular travel destinations.
Always verify requirements at the official embassy or consulate before travel, as rules
change frequently.

Last updated: 2024

---

JAPAN
-----
Visa-free countries: USA, UK, Canada, Australia, all EU member states, and 65+ others.
Duration: 90 days for tourism/business.
Requirements: Valid passport, return/onward ticket, proof of sufficient funds.
Visa required: China, India, Indonesia, and others — apply at Japanese embassy.
Note: Working holiday visas available for citizens of 30 countries (ages 18–30).

THAILAND
--------
Visa-free countries: USA, UK, Canada, Australia, Germany, France, and 90+ others.
Duration: 60 days for tourism (extended from 30 days in 2024).
Extension: Single 30-day extension available at any immigration office (~1,900 baht).
Visa on arrival: Available for 19 countries (includes India, China). 15 days. 2,000 baht fee.
Tourist visa (TR): 60 days, single or multiple entry, obtained from Thai embassy before travel.
Note: Thailand has reciprocal visa-free agreements — check current list as it updates frequently.

FRANCE (and Schengen Area)
--------------------------
EU/EEA citizens: No visa required, right of free movement.
Visa-free Schengen access: USA, UK, Canada, Australia, Japan, South Korea, and 60+ others.
Duration: 90 days within any 180-day period (across all 27 Schengen member states combined).
ETIAS: EU Travel Information and Authorisation System — expected 2025. Pre-travel
registration (~€7) will be required for currently visa-exempt travellers.
Long-stay visa: Required for stays over 90 days (study, work, family reunification).
Apply at French consulate/embassy minimum 3 months before travel.
UK post-Brexit: UK citizens can enter Schengen visa-free for 90/180 days but are no
longer EU citizens and are subject to passport checks at borders.

UNITED ARAB EMIRATES (Dubai)
-----------------------------
Visa on arrival / free visa: USA, UK, Canada, Australia, EU, Japan, and 50+ others.
Duration: 30 days on arrival, one extension of 30 days available online (~AED 600).
Some nationalities (GCC countries, select others): 90 days visa-free.
UAE tourist e-visa: Available online for other nationalities (30 or 90 days).
Israeli citizens: Can enter the UAE following the 2020 Abraham Accords normalisation.
Important: Entry may still be refused at immigration officer's discretion regardless
of visa status. Having a Dubai hotel booking and return ticket is strongly recommended.
Schengen visa holders (of certain nationalities): Can enter UAE with valid Schengen visa.

AUSTRALIA
---------
eVisitor (subclass 651): Free online visa for EU/UK/many European nationals. 90 days.
ETA (Electronic Travel Authority, subclass 601): AUD 20 fee. USA, Canada, Japan,
Singapore, South Korea, and others. 3 months per visit, multiple entry for 12 months.
Tourist visa (subclass 600): For other nationalities. AUD 145 fee. Online application.
Working Holiday (subclass 417/462): Available for 18–35 year olds from 44 countries.
Note: Australian immigration takes health and character requirements seriously. Declare
all goods on arrival. Biosecurity laws strictly enforced — heavy fines for undeclared food.

BALI / INDONESIA
----------------
Visa-free: 6 countries only (Brunei, Cambodia, Laos, Malaysia, Myanmar, Philippines, Thailand,
Singapore, Vietnam — ASEAN members).
Visa on arrival (VOA): Available to 95 countries including USA, UK, Australia, EU, Japan.
Cost: IDR 500,000 (~$32 USD). Duration: 30 days, extendable once for 30 days.
Note: Bali Social Visa announced — specific new rules for remote workers, check current status.
Important: Bali has rules against tourists working without a work permit. Digital nomads
should research the Indonesia E33G visa (announced but implementation ongoing as of 2024).

NEW ZEALAND
-----------
NZeTA (Electronic Travel Authority): Required for visa-waiver countries travelling by air.
Countries include USA, UK, Canada, Australia (different process), EU, Japan, and others.
Cost: NZD 23 online. Valid for multiple trips over 2 years. Plus IVL (International Visitor
Levy) of NZD 35 per trip, paid at same time.
Duration: Up to 3 months per visit.
Working holiday: Available to 18–30 year olds (35 for some countries) from 44 countries.

GENERAL TIPS FOR ALL DESTINATIONS
-----------------------------------
Passport validity: Most countries require your passport to be valid for at least 6 months
beyond your planned departure date. Check this before booking.
Onward/return ticket: Many countries require proof of onward or return travel. Even when
not legally required, border officials may ask.
Proof of funds: Some countries ask for bank statements showing sufficient funds.
A general guideline is USD 50–100 per day, but this varies widely.
Travel insurance: Not legally required for most tourist visas but strongly recommended.
Some countries (e.g., Cuba, certain Schengen entries) may require proof of insurance.
Dual nationals: Always enter and exit on the same passport when possible. Check whether
your nationality requires a visa even if your second passport does not.
Criminal record: Some countries (Australia, USA, Canada) may refuse entry to travellers
with criminal convictions. Declare honestly on entry forms.
